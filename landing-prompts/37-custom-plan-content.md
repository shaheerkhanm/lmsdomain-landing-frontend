# Custom Plan: page content (copy deck)

This is the starting copy for the Custom Plan offer. Prompt 37a puts every line below into the admin as editable demo content, and prompt 37b shows it on the site. Nothing here is hard-coded in the frontend: you can change any line in the admin later.

Written after studying how the strongest custom / white-label LMS providers sell (Innowise, Akveo, LearnWorlds, Teachfloor, Edmingle). What they all do: one outcome headline, a visible launch timeline, the deliverables listed one by one (Android and iOS as separate items), a "this vs. the usual way" comparison, a clear next step with a response time, and an FAQ that answers cost, timeline, ownership and support.

## Promises to clients (confirmed by Shaheer, 3 Nov)

These defaults were confirmed as written. Change them in the admin if they ever change.

1. Timeline: web academy live in **3 to 5 working days**, Android and iOS apps **submitted to the stores within 7 to 10 working days**. Store review time (Apple/Google) is outside your control, so the copy says "submitted", not "live".
2. Store accounts: apps published under the **client's own** Google Play and Apple developer accounts (client pays the store fees: Google one-time $25, Apple $99/year). If you publish under your account instead, change that line.
3. Response time: proposal and quote within **24 hours** of the enquiry.
4. Pricing: shown as "Quote based on your needs" (no number). If you want a "starting from" price, add it in the admin.
5. Services included: setup, branding, content upload help, data migration from an old LMS, payment gateway setup, admin training, hosting, updates and support. Remove any you don't offer.
6. Source code / ownership: the copy says the client owns their content, learner data, domain and store listings. It does **not** promise source code. Change if you do offer it.

## 10. FAQ

1. **How fast can my LMS really go live?** Your web academy is usually live within 3 to 5 working days after you approve the plan and send your logo, colours and domain details. Android and iOS apps are submitted to the stores within 7 to 10 working days; Apple and Google then review them, which usually takes a few days.
2. **Do I get my own apps on Google Play and the App Store?** Yes. The apps carry your name, icon and splash screen and are published under your own developer accounts, so the listings belong to you.
3. **What does a Custom Plan cost?** It depends on the number of learners, the apps you need, any migration and custom features. After a short call you get a written quote within 24 hours, with no hidden costs.
4. **Can you move my courses and learners from my current platform?** Yes. We migrate courses, videos, tests, learners and their progress from most LMS platforms, or from spreadsheets and folders.
5. **Who owns the content and learner data?** You do. Your courses, learner data, domain and store listings stay yours.
6. **Can you build features we need that aren't in the platform?** Yes. Tell us what you need on the call; we scope it, include it in the quote and give you a delivery date.
7. **What happens after launch?** We host your academy, keep it updated with new features, fix issues and help your team whenever needed.
8. **How is this different from your standard plans?** Standard plans let you set up the academy yourself. A Custom Plan is done for you: we set up, brand, migrate, publish your apps and build anything extra you need.
